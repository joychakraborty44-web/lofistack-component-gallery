DHAKA AFTER RAIN

rooftop cha stall · monsoon night
pixel scene · claude
Move to look · tap the bulbs, the cup, the radio & a passing rickshaw

A humble চায়ের দোকান under a tin awning, warm bulbs swaying over the counter while monsoon rain glazes the Dhaka street below — rickshaws rolling through puddles, kettle steam, birds on the wires. Drawn pixel by pixel, animated in real time.
