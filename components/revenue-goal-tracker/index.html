Revenue Goal Tracker

How close the team is to its revenue goal, and whether the current pace will get there. Log a sale and every number updates: progress, milestones, days left and the daily run-rate you now need.

Example data. Fictional clients, not real revenue.

Fields

goalTarget amount. Attribute or JSON.
start · endFirst and last day of the goal period, YYYY-MM-DD. Both days count.
todayOptional. The day progress is measured on (defaults to the real date, kept inside the period).
sales[]{ client, amount, date }. Amount raised is the sum of these.
milestonesPercentages to mark on the meter (default [25, 50, 75, 100]).
eyebrow · titleOptional header text.
currency · localeMoney and date formatting (default USD, en-US).
recentHow many sales to list (default 4).
labelsOverride any built-in text.

Run-rate is the amount raised divided by the days so far. Required rate is what is left divided by the days left. Projected is the current run-rate across the whole period.

Usage

<revenue-goal-tracker goal="120000">
  <script type="application/json">
    {
      "title": "New retainer revenue",
      "start": "2026-07-01",
      "end": "2026-09-30",
      "sales": [
        { "client": "Brightside Dental",
          "amount": 4800, "date": "2026-07-03" }
      ]
    }
  </script>
</revenue-goal-tracker>

// or from code:
el.addSale({ client: "Summit Physio", amount: 3600 });
el.undo();
el.addEventListener("goalprogress", e => e.detail);

Every added or undone sale fires goalprogress with the new total, percentage, the sale and any milestones just crossed. Crossing a milestone also shows a short announcement and, unless reduced motion is on, a small burst of confetti.
